Brad Freeman is a psychoanalytic psychotherapist and clinical psychologist based in Hobart, Tasmania, with over forty years’ experience in the mental health field. He works with children, adolescents, couples and families, in private practice and online, and has a longstanding interest in the application of psychoanalytic theory to contemporary culture. He also writes poetry and prose.
